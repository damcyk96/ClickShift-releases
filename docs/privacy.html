Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

ClickShift has no account, no analytics and no advertising, and it sends nothing to its developer. This page describes what the apps and this website do with data.

The apps

  • Bluetooth. ClickShift scans for Zwift Click controllers and connects to them to read button presses and battery level. Other Bluetooth devices are ignored. Nothing about them leaves your device.
  • Local network. ClickShift announces itself on your local network (Bonjour / mDNS) so that MyWhoosh can find it, and sends MyWhoosh the button presses and the battery level over that network. This traffic stays inside your network.
  • Settings. The Click mode, your preferences and a random identifier for this installation are stored on your device. The identifier lets MyWhoosh recognise ClickShift on the network; it is not sent to the developer.
  • Logs. The log is kept on your device and is only shared if you copy it yourself, for example into a bug report. It can contain local IP addresses and Bluetooth device identifiers, so review it before posting.
  • Update check (macOS and Windows). Desktop versions that offer updates ask GitHub once, when they start, for the number of the latest release. GitHub receives your IP address and the ClickShift version, as with any web request. See GitHub's privacy statement.

This website

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or trackers. Like any website, the service that hosts it can log technical data such as your IP address and browser type. The downloads are served by GitHub.

App stores

When you get ClickShift from the App Store or Google Play, Apple or Google process the download under their own privacy policies. ClickShift receives no personal data from them.

Bug reports

Bug reports are public issues on GitHub. Whatever you write or attach there is visible to everyone and handled under GitHub's terms.

Changes

If ClickShift starts to handle data differently, this page changes first and shows the new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue in the ClickShift downloads repository.
